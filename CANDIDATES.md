## Head-to-head among the top three (71 pts each)

* **#7 vs #4:** both on-subject and deadpan. #7's joke (snow falling
  *upwards*, anchor your washing) lands harder on first read than #4's
  glitter snow; #4 has one more number. Criterion 2 decides: **#7 wins.**
* **#8 vs #7:** #8's named cloud is the more surprising, more specific
  image — a city bureaucracy naming its one cloud — and beats upward snow
  on criteria 2 and 3. **#8 wins.**
* **#8 vs #4:** #8 funnier on first read (named cloud vs glitter snow).
  Criterion 2 decides: **#8 wins.**

Tournament winner: **#8**.

## Final polish of the winner

Two edits before freezing, neither changing the joke:

1. City renamed **New Rotterdam → Port Meridian**. "New Rotterdam" leans
   on a real city and could read as a real place with a prefix; Port
   Meridian is invented outright, which criterion 1 rewards.
2. "one cloud" → **"one (1) cloud"**. The parenthetical count reads like
   a municipal form — one more deadpan bureaucratic beat, same joke.
